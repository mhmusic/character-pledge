Show that you signed

Put it on your site, your press kit, your profile, your email. One line of code, or none at all.

Signing is a private act until somebody sees it. A badge on your own page does more than a name on a list here — it tells the people who visit you, on your ground, what you expect of anyone who learns from your work.

If you can't paste code

  • Bandcamp, Instagram, or anywhere with a bio field: use the plain-text line above.
  • A press kit or one-sheet: drop the badge image into the layout like any other logo.

Using it honestly

Use the badge if you've signed, and take it down if you change your mind. It says one thing: that you've stated where you stand. It doesn't claim a license, a contract, or anything about who you work with.
